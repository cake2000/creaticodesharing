## Introduction

In this tutorial, we'll have some fun playing with dancing aliens:

![ada838b6-d69d-41b0-9144-6957c1e27d36.gif](https://github.com/cake2000/creaticodesharing/blob/main/images/ada838b6-d69d-41b0-9144-6957c1e27d36.gif?raw=true)

&nbsp;
## Step 1 - Initialize A "Grass Land" Scene with 3D Axes

Please create a new project, and remove the dog sprite. In the "Empty1" sprite, add the green-flag block, the "[initialize 3D scene](/topic/5/initializing-a-3d-scene)" block, and the "add 3D axis" block. Note that you need to click the input box and choose the "Grass Land" scene. This is a lightweight scene with a large grassland and a blue sky.

![35e64a77-5128-4d98-9815-8751b283ad3c.gif](https://github.com/cake2000/creaticodesharing/blob/main/images/35e64a77-5128-4d98-9815-8751b283ad3c.gif?raw=true)


As shown, the X-axis points to the right, the Y-axis points forward (into the screen), and the Z-axis points up. You can drag your mouse on the stage to rotate the camera around it:

## Step 2 - Add an alien avatar

Avatars are human-like models that can perform various kinds of animations. Here is how to [add an alien avatar](/topic/11/loading-an-avatar-into-a-scene):

## Step 3 - Teach the alien to dance

The alien avatar only knows how to "idle". We need to "teach" it how to do some dancing animations by "[adding animations](/topic/12/avatar-animations)" to it. Here is how to browse through the animations and add the "Breakdance Ready Dance" animation.

![e5a2bb60-ba31-470a-8836-0ef7b24c10be.gif](https://github.com/cake2000/creaticodesharing/blob/main/images/e5a2bb60-ba31-470a-8836-0ef7b24c10be.gif?raw=true)

## Step 4 - Start the dance

Now after adding the dance animation, we can start the animation at any time. By default, the animation will restart after it completes one round, so it will keep going repeatedly.

![a4ee9c6e-5d30-4f80-b92e-540d7ac602c5.gif](https://github.com/cake2000/creaticodesharing/blob/main/images/a4ee9c6e-5d30-4f80-b92e-540d7ac602c5.gif?raw=true)

## Step 6 - Copy and Move

Next, let's add another alien avatar. We can [make a copy of it](/topic/110/copying-objects), and choose to share the data between these 2 avatars. This will make your program run faster, since all the avatars will reuse the same color and animation data.

We also need to move the copied avatar to a different position, otherwise, it will overlap with the original avatar.

## Creative Ideas

There are many ways you can extend this project. Here are some ideas for your inspiration:

1. **Changing Body Colors**: you can try to change the alien's body color randomly on each step.

2. **More Aliens**: you can try to add much more avatars, and move/rotate them to form an interesting formation.

3. **Make a New Scene**: you can switch to a new scene and create something more interesting. For example, this scene looks like the 2 aliens are arguing about whether to eat pizza:
